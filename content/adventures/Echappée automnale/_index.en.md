At the end of October, a cold black north wind rises over the Gironde and urges me to flee eastward in search of, I hope, a patch of blue sky and carpets of yellow leaves.
From the volcanic plateaus of the Cantal to the winding gorges of the Vercors, by way of the vast grassy expanses of the Aubrac, this improvised journey should put my perseverance to the test.
But I have no doubt it will be worth the effort.
